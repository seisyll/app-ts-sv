# Adding people to your workspace

Admins add people from **Users**, choose what each one may do, and can take that back at any time.

## Add someone

1. Open **Users** and choose **Add user**.
2. Enter their name and email, and pick a role.
3. Leave **Password** empty to send them an invite: they get an email with a link to choose their own password, valid for a week. Or set a password yourself and share it privately — they can change it later under **Password**.

Invites need the workspace's email to be set up (see [Setting up email](?help=email-setup)). Until it is, you choose the password.

## Roles

- **Admin** — everything, including people, appearance, email and the other settings.
- **Member** — the workspace itself: their own profile, the media library, notifications.

## Later

The **…** menu on someone's row changes their role, signs them out on every device, bans them (they can't sign in until unbanned), removes an uploaded picture, or deletes them. None of it applies to your own account, so a workspace always keeps the admin who made the change.
